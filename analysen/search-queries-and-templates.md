## Theme Tag Taxonomy

Use these standardized tags for consistency:

### Primary Themes
- **PAIN** - Current frustrations and problems
- **TRIGGER** - Events that cause action/need
- **OUTCOME** - Desired results and success metrics
- **ALTERNATIVE** - Other solutions being used
- **LANGUAGE** - Authentic vocabulary and phrases
- **OBJECTION** - Barriers to adoption
- **WORKFLOW** - Current processes and routines
- **SAFETY** - Risk and safety concerns

### Sub-Themes (Examples)

**Under PAIN:**
- Tool overload (too many apps)
- Decision uncertainty (don't know if safe)
- Time consumption (takes too long)
- Information conflict (sources disagree)
- Complexity (too technical)
- Inaccuracy (forecast wrong)
- Missing context (doesn't explain why)
- No Swiss specificity (not local enough)

**Under TRIGGER:**
- Morning planning routine
- Special conditions (Föhn alert)
- Accident report read
- Peer invitation to fly
- Weather window opportunity
- Progression milestone (solo flight)

**Under OUTCOME:**
- Confident decision
- Time saved
- Improved safety
- More flying days
- Faster skill development
- Peer respect/credibility

**Under ALTERNATIVE:**
- Windy app
- XCWeather
- MeteoSchweiz
- Webcams
- Peer consultation
- Instructor check-in
- Local knowledge

## Quick Extraction Process

### When Reading a Forum Thread:

1. **Skim first** - Identify which posts have substantial content (3+ sentences)
2. **Profile check** - Look at poster's profile for experience level clues
3. **Copy verbatim** - Don't paraphrase, copy exact words
4. **Capture context** - What question/situation prompted this?
5. **Tag immediately** - Assign themes while it's fresh
6. **Note confidence** - Prompted vs. unprompted? Emotional language? Detailed?
7. **Move on** - Don't over-analyze during extraction, save that for synthesis

### When Reading App Reviews:

1. **Filter to 1-3 stars** - Higher signal for pain points
2. **Skip generic complaints** - "Crashes too much" isn't useful unless it's weather-specific
3. **Look for "I wish"** - Feature requests are gold
4. **Look for comparison language** - "Better/worse than X"
5. **Look for use case descriptions** - "I use it for..."
6. **Check review date** - Apps change, weight recent reviews more

### When Reading Social Media:

1. **Daily decision threads** - High value for workflow insights
2. **Accident discussions** - High value for safety concerns
3. **Beginner questions** - High value for onboarding/learning pains
4. **Tool recommendation requests** - High value for competitive intel
5. **Skip pure social content** - "Great flight today!" with no weather context = skip
6. **Check comment threads** - Often more valuable than original posts
