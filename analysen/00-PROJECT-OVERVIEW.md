## Project Scope

### What You'll Research

**Primary Questions**:
1. How do Swiss pilots currently plan their flying days?
2. What are their biggest frustrations with weather forecasting?
3. What tools do they use and why are multiple tools needed?
4. How do beginners vs. experienced pilots differ in their needs?
5. What Swiss-specific challenges exist (Föhn, valley winds, airspace)?
6. What would make pilots trust and adopt a new AI weather tool?

**Target Segments**:
- Beginners (0-2 years) - Learning weather interpretation
- Intermediate (2-5 years) - Building independence
- Advanced (5+ years) - Established workflows, may be XC specialists
- Instructors - Teaching perspective, safety focus

**Geographic Focus**:
- Switzerland (primary)
- With comparison to general Alpine/international paragliding

---

### What You'll Create

**Deliverables**:
1. **Research Synthesis Report** - Top 15 themes with quotes and implications
2. **Customer Personas** - 3-4 detailed persona documents
3. **VOC Quote Bank** - 50+ best quotes organized for copywriting
4. **Competitive Analysis** - What pilots say about Windy, XCWeather, MeteoSchweiz
5. **Jobs-to-be-Done Map** - Functional, emotional, social jobs by segment

**Raw Materials**:
- 200-300 verbatim customer quotes
- Extraction spreadsheet with themes, segments, confidence levels
- Source documentation (URLs, dates, context)

## Research Methodology

### Approach: Digital Watering Hole Research

**Definition**: Finding and analyzing authentic customer conversations in online communities where they naturally gather.

**Why This Approach**:
- No existing customers to interview (pre-product)
- Pilots are active online (forums, Facebook, Reddit)
- Rich discussions about weather tools and frustrations available
- Unfiltered, authentic language (not interview polish)

### Sources to Research

**Primary** (highest value):
1. paraglidingforum.com - Switzerland weather discussions
2. Facebook groups - "Swiss Paragliding", "Gleitschirm Schweiz"
3. App reviews - Windy, XCWeather, MeteoSwiss (1-3 stars)
4. SHV-FSVL - Official Swiss association safety materials

**Secondary** (supporting):
5. Reddit - r/freeflight, r/paragliding
6. YouTube - Comments on Swiss paragliding videos, weather tutorials
7. XContest - Flight reports with weather notes

### Extraction Process

**For Each Quote**:
1. Copy verbatim (don't paraphrase)
2. Note source (platform, URL, date)
3. Capture context (what prompted the comment)
4. Identify pilot segment (beginner/intermediate/advanced)
5. Tag theme (PAIN, WORKFLOW, OUTCOME, etc.)
6. Assess confidence (high/medium/low)

**Quality Standards**:
- Minimum 200 quotes for basic synthesis
- Target 300+ quotes for comprehensive research
- At least 30 quotes per segment (beginner/intermediate/advanced)
- At least 50 quotes at HIGH confidence level
- Coverage across all major themes

## Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid

### Pitfall 1: Paraphrasing Quotes
**Problem**: Losing authentic voice, making quotes generic
**Solution**: Always copy exact words, even if grammar is imperfect

### Pitfall 2: Insufficient Segmentation
**Problem**: Averaging across all pilots, losing nuance
**Solution**: Always tag segment (beginner/intermediate/advanced)

### Pitfall 3: Confirmation Bias
**Problem**: Only seeing quotes that confirm your assumptions
**Solution**: Actively look for contradictions, different perspectives

### Pitfall 4: Tool Overload During Extraction
**Problem**: Spending too much time on complex extraction setup
**Solution**: Keep it simple - spreadsheet with standard columns

### Pitfall 5: Analysis Paralysis During Extraction
**Problem**: Over-thinking each quote, slowing down extraction
**Solution**: Tag quickly, move on - analysis happens in Week 4

### Pitfall 6: Ignoring Low-Signal Sources
**Problem**: Extracting from every post, including generic content
**Solution**: Be selective - only extract substantive content

### Pitfall 7: Starting Synthesis Too Early
**Problem**: Drawing conclusions before you have enough data
**Solution**: Hit 200-quote minimum before synthesis

### Pitfall 8: Not Documenting Confidence
**Problem**: Treating all insights as equally valid
**Solution**: Always label HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW confidence

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Can't Find Enough Swiss-Specific Content
**Likelihood**: Low (Swiss pilots are active online)
**Mitigation**:
- Use Swiss city names in searches (Interlaken, Engelberg)
- Join Swiss-specific Facebook groups
- Include international content for comparison

### Risk 2: Language Barrier (German/French Content)
**Likelihood**: Medium (majority of Swiss content is German)
**Mitigation**:
- Use Chrome auto-translate or DeepL
- Focus on English-language international forums first
- Many Swiss pilots write in English anyway

### Risk 3: Too Time-Consuming
**Likelihood**: Medium (can easily exceed time budget)
**Mitigation**:
- Set daily quote targets (20-30 per day)
- Use timer - move on after allocated time
- Sprint version available (1 week, 150 quotes)

### Risk 4: Research Becomes Outdated
**Likelihood**: Low (core needs change slowly)
**Mitigation**:
- Focus on timeless pains (not tool-specific complaints)
- Update quarterly with new extraction rounds
- Monitor key sources monthly for major shifts
