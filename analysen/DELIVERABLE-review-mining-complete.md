## 💡 Key Insights from Framework

### What Makes a Good Quote
- **Specific** (not generic)
- **Actionable** (implies solution)
- **Comparative** (mentions competitors)
- **Emotional** (strong sentiment)
- **Contextual** (explains use case)

### Priority Reviews to Read
1. 1-2 star reviews (highest pain signal)
2. 3-star reviews (most balanced/honest)
3. Long reviews (6+ sentences - most informative)
4. Recent reviews (last 6 months - most relevant)
5. Reviews mentioning competitors (comparative insights)

### Most Valuable Sources
1. **Reddit r/freeflight** - Honest comparative discussions
2. **App Store 1-2 star reviews** - Critical pain points
3. **YouTube comments** - User stories and experiences
4. **Forum discussions** - Deep technical feedback
