## 📈 Quote Quality Checklist

### ✅ Good Quote (Extract This)
- Specific detail ("wind was 20mph not 5mph")
- Actionable insight ("needs airspace warnings")
- Emotional ("this made me miss a flying day")
- Comparative ("XCTrack does this better")
- Use case explained ("when planning XC flights...")

### ❌ Bad Quote (Skip This)
- Generic ("great app!")
- Vague ("needs improvement")
- Irrelevant ("I like the icon")
- Obvious fake/spam

## 🎁 Bonus Tips

### Reddit Gold Mine
- r/freeflight is most active
- Search for comparative threads
- Users are honest (no incentive to lie)
- Look for "app recommendation" discussions

### App Store Insights
- Sort by "Most Critical" first
- 1-2 star reviews = highest pain
- 3-star reviews = most balanced
- Long reviews = most informative

### YouTube Comments
- Read comments on tutorial videos
- Users share real experiences
- Look for "I wish..." statements
- Check creator responses

### Time Savers
- Use Ctrl+F to search within reviews
- Focus on recent reviews (last 6 months)
- Skip generic "great app!" reviews
- Prioritize detailed complaints
