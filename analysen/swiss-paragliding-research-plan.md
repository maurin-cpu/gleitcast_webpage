# Swiss Paragliding Community Research Plan
## AI-Powered Weather App Customer Research

**Date Created**: April 17, 2026
**Project**: Wingcast (AI Paragliding Weather App)
**Research Mode**: Digital Watering Hole Research (Mode 2)
**Target Market**: Swiss Paragliding Pilots

---

## Research Objectives

1. Understand Swiss pilots' weather planning workflows and pain points
2. Identify safety concerns and risk assessment patterns
3. Map beginner vs. experienced pilot differences
4. Uncover Swiss-specific challenges (Föhn, alpine terrain, airspace)
5. Document current tools used and frustrations
6. Extract authentic language for messaging and copy

## Research Quality Standards

### Confidence Levels

| Level | Criteria | Minimum Data Points |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| **HIGH** | 5+ independent sources; unprompted; consistent across segments | 5+ |
| **MEDIUM** | 2-4 sources; prompted or limited to one segment | 2-4 |
| **LOW** | 1 source; potential outlier; needs validation | 1 |

### Sample Bias Awareness

- **Forum users** = Skew toward experienced, opinionated pilots
- **Facebook groups** = Mix of all levels, but self-selected engaged community
- **App reviews** = Skew toward frustrated users and power users
- **YouTube comments** = Skew younger, less experienced
- **SHV/FSVL materials** = Official view, safety-focused, may not reflect actual behavior

Do not assume these biases represent "all Swiss pilots" - flag them in the report.

### Minimum Viable Sample

- Don't build personas from <5 data points per segment
- Don't claim "Swiss pilots think X" without 10+ supporting quotes from 5+ independent sources
- Flag everything as HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW confidence

## Swiss-Specific Research Context

### Key Swiss Paragliding Characteristics to Validate

1. **Language diversity**: German, French, Italian regions - does weather culture differ?
2. **SHV/FSVL influence**: How much authority do official recommendations have?
3. **MeteoSchweiz trust**: Is the official weather service considered authoritative?
4. **Föhn expertise**: Do Swiss pilots have specialized Föhn knowledge vs. international pilots?
5. **Alpine terrain complexity**: How does mountain flying affect weather needs vs. flatland?
6. **Transport culture**: Does reliance on public transport shape flying decisions?
7. **Airspace density**: Are pilots more cautious due to military zones and TMA?
8. **Safety culture**: Is Swiss paragliding more conservative than other regions?

### Geographic Segmentation Possibility

Consider analyzing by region:
- **Bernese Oberland** (Interlaken, Grindelwald) - Tourism, high volume
- **Central Switzerland** (Lucerne, Engelberg) - Mix of locals and tourists
- **Ticino** (Italian-speaking, Mediterranean influence)
- **Romandie** (French-speaking, Lausanne area)
- **Eastern Switzerland** (St. Gallen, less alpine)

If patterns differ significantly by region, flag this for product localization.
