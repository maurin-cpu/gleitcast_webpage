## Common Questions

### Q: Do I really need 200+ quotes?
**A:** Yes, for confidence in patterns. 50 quotes = hypotheses. 200 quotes = validated insights.

### Q: How long does one quote take?
**A:**
- Fast: 5 min (just basics)
- Good: 10 min (full context + analysis)
- Gold: 15 min (deep insights + connections)

### Q: What if I can't find quotes on a topic?
**A:** That's a finding! "No one complains about X" is valuable data.

### Q: Should I extract positive quotes too?
**A:** YES! "I love app X because Y" tells you what to build/copy.

### Q: Do German quotes need translation?
**A:** Keep original in `verbatim_quote`, add translation in `translation` field.

### Q: Can I use App Store reviews?
**A:** Yes! They're in the checklist. Great source of specific complaints.

### Q: How do I know if a quote is good?
**A:** Good quotes are:
- Specific (numbers, names, workflows)
- Emotional (frustrated, scared, excited)
- Actionable (tells you what to build/fix)
- Authentic (real pilot voice)

## Pro Tips

### Finding Gold Quotes
- Look for stories ("Last week I...")
- Look for workflows ("First I check X, then Y...")
- Look for numbers ("45 minutes", "5 apps", "€120")
- Look for emotion ("terrified", "exhausted", "love")

### Efficient Research
- Set timer: 30 min research, 10 min break
- Batch similar searches (all beginner searches together)
- Extract while fresh (don't save threads for later)
- Use browser tabs: Keep template open in one tab

### Quality Control
- Re-read your first 10 quotes after a day
- Compare to examples - are you capturing enough detail?
- Ask: "Could I build a feature from this quote?"
- Ask: "Could I write marketing copy from this?"

---

## When You're Stuck

### "I can't find any good quotes"
- Try different search terms
- Go broader (search "paragliding weather" not "paragliding weather app AI")
- Try different platforms (Reddit → Forum → App Reviews)
- Read most popular threads, not just search results

### "This quote seems vague"
- Don't extract it - move on
- Quality > quantity
- Only extract when there's actionable insight

### "I don't know how to tag this"
- Tag generously (multiple tags OK)
- When in doubt, add the tag
- You can cluster later

### "The format feels tedious"
- It is! But it's worth it
- Detailed extraction = easy analysis later
- Shortcuts now = confusion later
