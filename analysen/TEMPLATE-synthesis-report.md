## Sample Bias Analysis

### Forum User Bias
- **Skew**: More experienced, more opinionated, more technical
- **Not representative of**: Casual pilots, less engaged pilots
- **Corrected by**: Including Facebook (broader range), YouTube (beginner-heavy)

### App Review Bias
- **Skew**: Frustrated users, power users
- **Not representative of**: Satisfied users (don't leave reviews)
- **Corrected by**: Noting this in analysis, not over-weighting complaints

### Geographic Bias
- **Overrepresented**: Interlaken/Bernese Oberland (most content)
- **Underrepresented**: Eastern Switzerland, Graubünden (less content)
- **Implication**: Findings skew toward high-Alpine, high-traffic areas
